### 2023 — Starting a New Chapter

Eight years ago, in December 2014, I published my first blog post on Medium, to give an account of [Smart Contracts](https://heckerhut.medium.com/whats-a-smart-contract-in-search-of-a-consensus-c268c830a8ad?source=user_profile---------10----------------------------) and their meaning from a legal perspective. Albeit being quite explorative, that post has been shared and read tens of thousands of times across the Internet and hopefully helped some fellow lawgens get excited about [blockchain and law](https://trends.google.de/trends/explore?date=2014-12-12%202022-12-28&q=blockchain%20law).

Not only did I discover that I really enjoyed blogging back then but I became obsessed with thinking deeply about what crypto and blockchain would mean for an information-based society. In the two years following my first blog post, I wrote about

*   [modes of contractual governance in an on-demand service economy](https://medium.com/p/1833629f379b?source=post_stats_page-------------------------------------) (2015), proposing on-chain social graphs and reputation systems to solve platform lock-in for workers in networked digital environments, a phenomenon that’s just now taking off;
*   about [the political economy of computing](https://medium.com/p/7fffc75423a2?source=post_stats_page-------------------------------------) (2015), scoping new forms of regulation in the age of platforms, now a hot topic with the EU’s [Digital Market Act](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/priorities-2019-2024/europe-fit-digital-age/digital-markets-act-ensuring-fair-and-open-digital-markets_en);
*   about [smart contracts, platforms and intermediaries](https://medium.com/p/c3d30f5182a6?source=post_stats_page-------------------------------------) (2015), where I predicted MEV in the Ethereum blockchain, [5 years before it arose](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2020/08/ethereum-is-a-dark-forest);
*   and about [a blockchain token taxonomy](https://medium.com/p/fadf5c56139a?source=post_stats_page-------------------------------------) (2016), where I explained why tokens were the single most important social innovation in blockchain, foreshadowing NFTs, and some reasoning why the Metaverse will only become real with blockchains, now being discussed as web4 — or the spatial web among researchers.

Looking back, I’m quite proud of these reflections, since they essentially captured the evolution of the crypto space for the years to come. You could say I was ahead of my time.

### Get ready for the next chapter 📖

The internet has become [political](https://stratechery.com/2021/internet-3-0-and-the-beginning-of-tech-history/). Social movements are the new change agents, fundamentally transforming work, politics, community, how we live and everything else.

That’s why I’ve decided to build [Common Ground](https://commonground.cg/) - a web3-powered platform for collectives of all kinds, helping them grow and collaborate.

Behind Common Ground stands an incredible team of builders, investors and communities, that are united by a shared vision of a better internet.
