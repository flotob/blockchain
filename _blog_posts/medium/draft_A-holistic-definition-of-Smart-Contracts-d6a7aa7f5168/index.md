## Smart contracts

### … and the two phases of contracting

  

In my [last post](https://medium.com/@heckerhut/whats-a-smart-contract-in-search-of-a-consensus-c268c830a8ad) I closely followed Nick Szabo’s definition of smart contracts to elaborate on the meaning of the term. In this post I’d like to give you a wider picture of the concept. To accomplish this, we will have to venture outside the boundaries of what might be called the “Crypto Scene”\[1\]. 

This wouldn’t be a decent article on smart contracts though, if it wouldn’t start with a reference to [Nick Szabo](http://szabo.best.vwh.net/phases.html). In “phases of contracting” Szabo proposes the following model of the **temporal phases of contracting:**

**1) Ex-Ante**  
 Search  
 Negotiation  
 Commitment

**2) Ex-Post**  
 Performance  
 Adjudication

Before, I only looked at the second phase of contracting, i.e. what happens _after_ a contract has been closed (“_ex-post_”)\[2\]. However, there lies a big land full of exciting mysteries in the _ex-ante_ phase of contracting as well: 

1.  How can parties with complementary needs find each other?
2.  How can parties negotiate the terms of an agreement?
3.  How is the agreement transformed from its intangible state as a ‘platonic agreement’ into the language and medium of the execution environment?

* * *

Szabo resembled those 3 questions with the terms ‘search’, ‘negotiation’ and ‘commitment’. Each one is a separate challenge that touches upon a multitude of fields of research which I intend to introduce to you as much as I am capable.\[3\]
