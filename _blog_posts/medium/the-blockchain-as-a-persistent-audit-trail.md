### The Blockchain as a persistent audit trail

Today, startup culture is well and alive almost anywhere you go. Living in Berlin is especially exciting, since there is a major push towards building out a vivid ecosystem of capable entrepreneurs, engineers and designers as well as readily accessible streams of venture capital.

A functioning startup ecosystem though is strongly darwinistic: a sheer infinite number of companies competes for a finite amount of resources, be it user attention or venture capital. 

Just a tiny percentile of the startups being built today currently incorporate the concept of a trusted and persistent data store. Sure, Amazon advertises its compliance with a number of industry security standards that make their services more and help keep attackers at bay. But what about the startup itself?
