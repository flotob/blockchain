### Smart contracts and the two phases of contracting

  

Talking about [smart contracts](#), there are different perspectives of looking at the concept and its wider implications. One that is probably fairly overlooked by the people who develop the infrastructure to safely execute such software-driven contracts, is the **_other phase of contracting,_** the one preceding the binding commitment to a specific formalized agreement.

As [Szabo points out](#), there are two phases of contracting:

  

**1) Ex-Ante**  
 Search  
 Negotiation  
 Commitment

**2) Ex-Post**  
 Performance  
 Adjudication
