# Looking Ahead

- **Deepening engagement** with EU policymakers on upcoming digital asset legislation
- **Expanding outreach** to new member states for more inclusive and localized initiatives
- **Creating guidelines** for next-generation decentralized applications (dApps)
## Opportunities for Collaboration

- **Join our advocacy efforts** by participating in public consultations
- **Contribute to research** on data protection, identity solutions, and smart contracts
- **Sponsor educational events** to broaden industry understanding
## Call to Action

We invite all **stakeholders, innovators, and advocates** to collaborate with us. By working together, we can ensure that Europe remains a leader in shaping the global crypto narrative—responsibly, inclusively, and for the benefit of all.
