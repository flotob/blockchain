## Impact & Achievements

We have prevented many missteps in Brussels with regards to crypto regulation and successfully pushed for innovative regulation around decentralized use cases such as DeFi and DAOs.

### Public Policy

- **Digital Finance Package (MiCA, DORA, DLT Pilot Regime, TFR, and others)**
  - exempted sufficiently decentralized use cases through Recital 22 MiCA
  - established technological neutrality in many places
  - prevent the ban of Bitcoin in the EU
  - removed damaging rules around exchange settlement
- **Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) Package**
  - prevented the ban of self-custodial wallets in the EU
  - prevented KYC requirement for transactions between self-custodial wallets
- **European Data Strategy**
  - created industry awareness around smart contract specific regulation in the Data Act and organized a unified industry response to EU regulators
### Community Building

- organized dozens of events during important events in the ethereum ecosystem
- gave dozens of talks at important community events such as Devcon, EthCC, Devconnect, EthDenver, Edge City and many others
- created many events that brought together regulators, industry reps, devs, law students
- organized regulator-specific events and workshops at the national, EU and international level
### Public Goods Funding

- received many forms of public goods funding for our work
- raised funds from the biggest crypto native orgs in the industry
