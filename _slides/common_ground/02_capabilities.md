# Platform Capabilities

- **Decentralized Governance:** Empowering users with decision-making authority.
- **On-Chain Identity:** Seamless integration of web3 identities for secure interactions.
- **Token Gating:** Exclusive access to content and features through token ownership.
- **Real-Time Communication:** Chats, video calls, and live broadcasts to connect communities.
- **Community Wallets:** Shared treasuries for collective growth and investment.
