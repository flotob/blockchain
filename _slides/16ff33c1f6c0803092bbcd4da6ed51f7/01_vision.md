> Imagine a world where users own the platforms they use.

That’s the claim with which Ethereum originally nerdsniped me into its ecosystem, as a freshly baked law graduate and recent member of the Berlin bar back in 2014. 

While Ethereum’s vision has come true for financial platforms like Maker, Uniswap and Aave, who are all DAO-owned and governed and thus arguably user-owned, the vast majority of digital services we use today are still firmly in the hands of data brokers like Meta and Alphabet.

Common Ground is a community-owned and goverend social app that seamlessly integrates powerful web3 capabilities for individuals and groups to act onchain. Primitives like identity, roles and governance are woven into higher-level objects, to build an interconnected, antifragile network of communities that inherits the availability and security guarantees of the underlying decentralized blockchain architecture.

### Communication is the atomic unit of coordination

Common Ground empowers communities regardless of their technical skills to coordinate around shared purpose and take advantage of burgeoning onchain economies to accomplish their objectives. At no prior point in crypto’s 15 year history have all the primitives been available that Common Ground relies upon. 

### Social is where tech and culture meet

Common Ground consumes category leading protocols and embeds them into a hypersocial, community-centric context. Aggregating users across ecosystems, offering a best-in-class mobile & desktop experience, with web3-features seamlessly baked in.
