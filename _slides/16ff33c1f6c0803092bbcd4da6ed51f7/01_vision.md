# Bringing communities onchain

Common Ground is community-oriented social app that seamlessly integrated powerful web3 capabilities for individuals and groups to act onchain. Primitives like identity, roles and governance are woven into higher-level objects, to build an interconnected, antifragile network of communities that inherits the availability and security guarantees of the underlying decentralized blockchain architecture.

### **Coordination**

Communication is the atomic unit of coordination. Common Ground starts out with a real-time web3 communications architecture, to serve as the integration layer on which emerging onchain primitives like smart wallets, community tokens, onchain treasuries, governance and more are woven into a coherent, impactful interaction surface.

Hence, the application archetype that we chose as Common Ground’s foundation is that of Discord/Slack, i.e. group-based real-time communication. To differentiate from these established platforms, we’re integrating onchain protocols natively into the application, facilitating user experiences that Discord’s bot integrations cannot emulate.

Among the aspects that make Common Ground unique are:

- **industry-leading UI & UX** that’s easy to understand & navigate for all humans
- **progressive web app architecture**, runs as native app on mobile & desktop
- **native push notifications** for mobile & desktop devices
- **passkey-based onboarding** with zero-click smart wallet on favorite ecosystem chain
- **custom roles & granular permissions** in communities, including token-gated access
- **proof of personhood** and other onchain credentials for additional access-gating
- **community-owned accounts** governed by votes of onchain roles

### ***Convergence***

Apps will tend to aggregate end users and commoditize blockspace over time. Common Ground empowers communities regardless of their technical skills to coordinate around shared purpose and take advantage of burgeoning onchain economies to accomplish their objectives. At no prior point in crypto’s 15 year history have all the primitives been available that Common Ground relies upon. That includes

- **zero-friction on-ramping** with minimal mental transaction cost for end users
- **emergence of smart contract standards** for
- **cheap and available blockspace** on some broadly-supported VM standards
- **global stablecoin adoption**, with millions of people relying on them as MoP & SoV
- **DEXs and DeFi lending markets** with billions in liquidity
- complementary, centralized and decentralized **proof of personhood approaches**
- emergence of **onchain culture and communities** coordinating around coinship
- broad support of **PWAs with native push notifications** across all major mobile OS
Common Ground consumes category leading protocols and embeds them into a hypersocial, community-centric context. Aggregating users across ecosystems, offering a best-in-class mobile & desktop experience, with web3-features seamlessly baked in.
